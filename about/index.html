About Project 55 Motors

Independent used-car specialist in South Shropshire, focused on carefully selected vehicles prepared to a high standard.

Each vehicle is chosen with close attention to service history, ownership, MOT record and overall condition. If it isn’t right, it won’t be retailed.

Before sale, every car receives a thorough inspection, professional valet, and a clear listing — including full specification, preparation notes and high-quality photography.

The goal is a calm, professional buying experience built on trust and long-term reputation. Every vehicle is supplied with two keys.
