About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors exists for people who want a great car buying experience without the usual dealership noise. We focus on carefully selected cars, prepared to a high standard, with a straightforward, no-pressure approach.

How we work

We operate on a simple principle: do the job properly, communicate clearly, and let the car speak for itself. That means being selective about what we buy, being honest about what we find, and preparing each vehicle to a consistent standard.

The result is a smaller, curated stock list — but each car is there for a reason. If a vehicle doesn’t meet the brief, it doesn’t make the cut.

Preparation standards

Every vehicle follows a consistent prep routine. Some cars need very little, others need more — but the goal is the same: present the car honestly, and get it to the best condition it reasonably can be.

  • Multi-point inspection and road test
  • Service items addressed where due or needed
  • Professional valet, with deep cleaning where required
  • Paintwork enhanced (as appropriate) to restore clarity and finish
  • Documentation and history reviewed and presented clearly
  • 2 keys supplied wherever applicable
