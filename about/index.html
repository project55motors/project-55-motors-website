About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors exists for people who want a great car buying experience without the usual dealership noise. We focus on carefully selected cars, prepared to a high standard, with a clear, no-pressure approach.

What we do

We source a small selection of vehicles and aim to present them with the same level of care you’d expect from a premium brand. The goal is simple: less volume, more attention.

Each listing includes clear photos and practical detail so you can make an informed decision before you travel.

  • Carefully selected vehicles with strong history and condition.
  • Prepared to a high standard before advertising and viewing.
  • Clear communication and straightforward buying process.
