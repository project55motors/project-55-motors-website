Privacy

We keep this simple. We only use personal information to respond to enquiries and to complete a vehicle sale.

  • What we collect: details you send us (name, email, phone, message) and any information needed to complete a purchase.
  • Why: to reply to you, arrange viewings, handle part-exchange discussions, and complete paperwork.
  • Sharing: only where necessary (e.g. finance or warranty provider) and only for your transaction.
  • Retention: we keep records required for legal/accounting purposes.
  • Your choices: you can ask what data we hold and request correction.

If you have a privacy question, contact nathan@project55motors.co.uk.
