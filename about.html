About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors was created with a clear philosophy: offer exceptionally well-maintained, enjoyable-to-drive vehicles with total transparency and premium presentation — without dealership nonsense.

Every car is carefully selected, test-driven, inspected, and prepared to a standard we are proud to put our name on. If it wouldn’t be good enough for us personally, it will not appear in our stock.

We operate flexibly across Shropshire, and viewings are available by appointment. As a sole trader operation, every detail — from preparation to paperwork — is personally handled, ensuring you receive a premium, trustworthy, and enjoyable experience.
