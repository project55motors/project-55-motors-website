About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors is a small, independent used car business based in South Shropshire, focused on quality, transparency and an enjoyable driving experience.

Every vehicle is hand-picked, with particular attention paid to service history, previous ownership, MOT record and overall condition. The aim is simple: to offer cars we would confidently recommend to friends and family.

Before sale, each car receives a thorough check, professional valet and clear, honest presentation – including detailed descriptions and high-quality photography – so you know exactly what you are buying.

The long-term goal behind Project 55 is sustainable growth: a small, trusted operation that does things properly, without pressure or gimmicks. If a car isn’t right, it won’t be offered for sale.
