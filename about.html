About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors is a premium used vehicle retailer based in South Shropshire, focused on reliability, transparency and exceptional customer service.

Every vehicle is personally sourced, inspected and prepared to a high standard to ensure our customers receive maximum confidence and peace of mind. We only offer vehicles we would happily own ourselves.

Our aim is to deliver a calm, straightforward buying experience: honest advice, clear history, and cars that are genuinely ready to drive away.
