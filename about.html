About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors is an independent used car retailer based in South Shropshire. Our philosophy is simple: offer high-quality, well-maintained vehicles with total transparency.

Every car is hand-selected, history-checked, fully inspected and prepared to a high standard. We aim to provide the same level of trust, consistency and professionalism found at premium main-dealer showrooms — but with a personal touch.

Whether you're buying your first car, something economical, or a well-looked-after family vehicle, we are here to provide honest advice and a pressure-free experience.

Project 55 Motors is built around reliability, quality and long-term customer care. We want every owner to feel confident in their purchase — and enjoy every mile.
