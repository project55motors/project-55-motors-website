About Project 55 Motors

At Project 55 Motors in South Shropshire, we believe buying a car should be simple, honest and enjoyable.

We only source and sell cars that we would be happy to own ourselves. That principle guides every decision we make, ensuring a trustworthy, premium experience for every customer.

We specialise in low-mileage, well-documented cars. Every vehicle receives a thorough mechanical inspection, professional valet and full preparation before sale.
