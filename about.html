About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors is a premium used vehicle retailer based in Shropshire, dedicated to providing carefully selected, high-quality pre-owned cars. We focus on reliability, transparency and exceptional customer service.

Every vehicle is personally sourced, inspected, and prepared to a high standard to ensure our customers receive maximum confidence and peace of mind. Our aim is to raise the bar for what small independent dealerships can deliver.

Whether you’re looking for your first car, a family vehicle, an economical commuter or something special, we’re here to help you find the right match — with honest advice and a pressure-free experience.
