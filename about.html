About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors was founded with a simple goal: to offer high-quality, well-maintained vehicles that customers can buy with complete confidence.

Based in the scenic South Shropshire hills, we hand-select every vehicle based on condition, maintenance history, and overall driving quality. Our aim is to provide the experience of a premium dealership while remaining approachable, transparent, and customer-focused.

Every vehicle we offer is thoroughly checked, photographed clearly, and accurately described. We pride ourselves on honesty, attention to detail, and ensuring each customer is completely satisfied before, during, and after their purchase.

Whether you're looking for your first car, a reliable family vehicle, or something special, we are here to help you find the right match.
