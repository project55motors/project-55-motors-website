About Project 55 Motors

Project 55 Motors was founded with a simple goal: to provide high-quality used cars that are reliable, carefully chosen, and transparently presented.

Based in the heart of South Shropshire, we take pride in sourcing vehicles with excellent maintenance history, strong specifications and responsible previous ownership. Every car is carefully inspected and prepared so that customers can buy with confidence.

Whether you're purchasing your first car or upgrading to something special, our aim is to offer a respectful, helpful and pressure-free experience from enquiry to handover.
